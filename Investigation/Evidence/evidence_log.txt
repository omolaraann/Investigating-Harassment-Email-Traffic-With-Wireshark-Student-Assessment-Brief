------------------------------------------------------------
KEY FORENSIC LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

1. The case scenario describes an open, passwordless wireless
   network that could be accessed by multiple people.

2. The shared IP address 192.168.15.4 therefore cannot independently
   identify the human sender.

3. The MAC address identifies the observed network interface/device
   but does not establish the physical operator.

4. The browser cookie provides session/account evidence but does not
   independently prove who was physically operating the device.

5. The available PCAP does not independently provide endpoint login,
   device ownership, wireless association, or other direct evidence
   identifying the physical operator.
