CASE STUDY 1: INVESTIGATING HARASSMENT EMAIL TRAFFIC WITH WIRESHARK

QUESTION 8 - FINAL ATTRIBUTION ASSESSMENT
============================================================

Question:
What conclusion can be defended from the available evidence?

------------------------------------------------------------
EXECUTIVE ATTRIBUTION FINDING
------------------------------------------------------------

The available network evidence establishes a strong evidentiary
association between the harassment submission, the observed network
interface/device, the browser/session account jcoachj@gmail.com, and
Johnny Coach, who is listed as a Chemistry 109 student in the
case-provided investigation material.

However, the available PCAP evidence does not conclusively establish
that Johnny Coach was the physical person operating the device at
the exact time the harassment message was transmitted.

The appropriate forensic conclusion is therefore:

STRONG EVIDENTIARY ASSOCIATION, BUT NOT CONCLUSIVE PHYSICAL
ATTRIBUTION.

------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THE ATTRIBUTION IS NOT CONCLUSIVE
------------------------------------------------------------

Limitation 1 - Shared Open Wireless Network

The case scenario states that the dormitory wireless router was
open and passwordless.

Multiple people could therefore access the network.

The shared IP address 192.168.15.4 cannot independently identify a
specific human sender.

Limitation 2 - MAC Address Does Not Identify the Human Operator

The MAC address identifies the network interface/device observed in
the capture.

It does not establish which person was physically operating the
device at the time of the event.

Limitation 3 - Browser Cookie Does Not Prove Physical Operation

The Gmail cookie provides browser/session-level evidence associated
with jcoachj@gmail.com.

A browser session or authenticated account may potentially be used
by another person with access to the device or session.

Therefore, the cookie strengthens the attribution but does not by
itself prove the physical operator.

------------------------------------------------------------
CONFIDENCE ASSESSMENT
------------------------------------------------------------

Device-to-harassment association:
HIGH

The actual harassment POST is directly observed in Frame 83601 and
contains the relevant message.

Device-to-browser/account association:
HIGH

Frame 79187 shows the same source MAC/IP and the Gmail account
identifier.

Account-to-Chemistry-109-student association:
HIGH

The case-provided investigation material associates the account with
Johnny Coach and lists Johnny Coach on the Chemistry 109 roster.

Physical attribution to Johnny Coach:
MODERATE

The combined evidence strongly associates Johnny Coach with the
observed device/browser session, but the available network evidence
does not independently establish that he was physically operating
the device when the message was transmitted.

Overall attribution assessment:
STRONG ASSOCIATION, NOT CONCLUSIVE PHYSICAL ATTRIBUTION

------------------------------------------------------------
ADDITIONAL EVIDENCE THAT COULD STRENGTHEN ATTRIBUTION
------------------------------------------------------------

The following evidence could strengthen or independently confirm
the physical attribution:

- Endpoint operating-system login records
- User account authentication records
- Browser history and local browser artifacts
- DHCP lease records
- Wireless access-point association logs
- Device ownership or inventory records
- File-system and user-profile artifacts
- Independent witness or access-control evidence
- Additional network traffic linking the user to the device at the
  exact event time

------------------------------------------------------------
FINAL CONCLUSION
------------------------------------------------------------

The network capture provides strong evidence that the harassment
message was transmitted from client IP 192.168.15.4 using the
observed network interface with MAC address 00:17:f2:e2:c0:ce.

The same device/interface was observed in browser/session traffic
containing the Gmail account identifier jcoachj@gmail.com. The
case-provided investigation material associates this account with
Johnny Coach and identifies Johnny Coach as a student in Lily
Tuckrige's Chemistry 109 class.

Taken together, these findings provide a strong evidentiary
association between Johnny Coach and the device/browser session
used in the harassment activity.

However, because the dormitory wireless network was open and the
available evidence does not independently establish who physically
operated the device at the exact time of transmission, the evidence
does not support an absolute or conclusive statement that Johnny
Coach was the physical sender.

The defensible forensic conclusion is therefore:

The evidence strongly associates Johnny Coach with the device and
browser session from which the harassment message was transmitted,
but the available PCAP evidence alone is insufficient to establish
conclusive physical attribution beyond reasonable alternative
explanations.
