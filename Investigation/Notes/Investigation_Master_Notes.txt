1. INVESTIGATION OBJECTIVE
============================================================

The objective of this investigation is to examine the supplied
Nitroba network capture and determine whether the captured traffic
provides evidence linking the harassment message to a client
system, network device, browser/session account, and a possible
Chemistry 109 student.

The investigation distinguishes between network-level attribution,
device-level attribution, browser/session or account-level
association, and human attribution.

The evidence must not be interpreted beyond what the PCAP and
case-provided material can support.

3. INVESTIGATION APPROACH
============================================================

The investigation followed a progressive correlation approach.

1. Identify the relevant web service.
2. Identify the client communicating with the service.
3. Isolate the HTTP POST containing the harassment message.
4. Identify the Ethernet source MAC associated with the POST.
5. Examine traffic from the same MAC/IP combination for
   browser/session information.
6. Identify the relevant browser cookie and account identifier.
7. Compare the account/session evidence with the case-provided
   Chemistry 109 roster.
8. Assess attribution strength and limitations.

11. SHARED NETWORK LIMITATION
============================================================

The case scenario states that the dorm room network included an
open/passwordless wireless router.

Consequently, multiple people could potentially use the same
network connection.

The shared IP address 192.168.15.4 therefore cannot by itself
identify the human sender.

The MAC address provides stronger device-level correlation but
still does not establish who physically operated the device.

============================================================
12. ATTRIBUTION ASSESSMENT
============================================================

Device-to-harassment association:
HIGH

Device-to-browser/account association:
HIGH

Account-to-Chemistry-109-student association:
MODERATE

Physical attribution to Johnny Coach:
MODERATE

Overall assessment:

STRONG ASSOCIATION, NOT CONCLUSIVE PHYSICAL ATTRIBUTION

The evidence supports an association between the harassment
transmission, the observed network interface/device, the browser
session/account identifier jcoachj@gmail.com, and the case-provided
Chemistry 109 student Johnny Coach.

However, the PCAP alone does not establish that Johnny Coach was the
person physically operating the device when the message was
transmitted.

============================================================
13. ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS / LIMITATIONS
============================================================

1. Another person may have accessed the open wireless network.

2. Another person may have used the observed device.

3. Another person may have used an already-authenticated browser
   session.

4. The account identifier may not independently establish the
   physical identity of the person using the device.

5. The PCAP does not provide direct endpoint authentication or
   operating-system login evidence.

============================================================
14. ADDITIONAL EVIDENCE THAT COULD STRENGTHEN ATTRIBUTION
============================================================

Potential additional evidence includes:

- Operating-system login records
- Local user-account information
- Browser history and local browser artifacts
- Device ownership records
- DHCP records
- Wireless access-point association logs
- Authentication records
- File-system artifacts
- Endpoint security telemetry
- Additional network traffic
- Witness or access-control evidence

Such evidence could help distinguish device ownership, account
ownership, and actual physical operation.

============================================================
15. FINAL INVESTIGATIVE POSITION
============================================================

The strongest technical finding is that the harassment message was
transmitted from client IP 192.168.15.4 using Ethernet source MAC
00:17:f2:e2:c0:ce.

The same MAC/IP combination is associated with browser/session
traffic containing the account identifier jcoachj@gmail.com.

The case-provided Chemistry 109 roster lists Johnny Coach as a
student in Lily Tuckrige's class.

Taken together, these findings provide a strong evidentiary
association with Johnny Coach, but they do not provide conclusive
proof that Johnny Coach was the physical operator responsible for
sending the harassment message.

The appropriate forensic conclusion is therefore:

STRONG ASSOCIATION, NOT CONCLUSIVE PHYSICAL ATTRIBUTION.
