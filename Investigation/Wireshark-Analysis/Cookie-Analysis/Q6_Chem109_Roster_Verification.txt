------------------------------------------------------------
FORENSIC LIMITATION
------------------------------------------------------------

The roster establishes that Johnny Coach is a member of Chemistry
109. It does not, by itself, establish that Johnny Coach physically
operated the device at the time of the harassment submission.

The evidence should therefore be described as an attribution
association rather than definitive proof of the physical operator.

The device, browser/session, account, roster, and network evidence
must be considered together.
